San Francisco to Big Sur

A walk down the coast, in mid-April. Come for a day, come for the week.

A walk down the California coast in mid-April. Five days or so. San Francisco to Big Sur, by way of Santa Cruz, Monterey, and Carmel Valley.

This is a personal trip that I invite people to. It started in 2025. The 2026 walk happened. The 2027 walk is being planned.

The inspirations: the Camino. David Whyte's walking tours. Derek Sivers's walk-and-talks. Being alive, in nature, with good company.

The walks so far

  • 2025 . The first one. Six days. Vipul walked the whole trip; Ruben came for Big Sur. San Francisco to Fernwood.
  • 2026 . The second one. Four days, solo. Henry Cowell, Marina Beach, Tan Bark.
  • 2027 . Being planned. Five nights in mid-April. If you're reading this and want to come, say so.

What this is

Not a retreat. Not a race. Not a guided tour.

A walk. A small group. A practice for finding: the people, the land, the conversations, and whatever you brought with you that the trail was going to give back.
